# Episode 76 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex hauls first—no hesitation, no poetry—just the hard, procedural mercy of doing the next right thing fast. Mila slips in after him, cassette deck clutched tight, its spindles whispering like a nervous insect. Nolan backs in last, eyes still on the smoke, and Alex throws his shoulder into the hatch. The door swings shut with a deep, coffin-thick THUNK. Alex finds the interior wheel latch—paint flaked, knurled steel—and cranks until it bites. The mechanism answers with a ratcheting clack-clack-clack that feels like the bunker remembering how to be sealed. Outside, the Analog Order’s rhythm hits the other side of the steel a moment later—muted now, frustrated. Smoke still tries to creep through the seam… but the gasket swells and holds....
