# Episode 14 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex chooses the most dangerous weapon in the wastes: normal. He steps out onto the shimmering slag with his hands visible and his shoulders loose, walking like a man who’s spent his whole life crossing bad neighborhoods without giving them the satisfaction of fear. The ground crunches—glass and old storefront teeth—broadcasting your presence in a thousand tiny snaps. Mila and Nolan follow half a step behind, close enough to look like a single scavenging unit, not a tactical team. Mila keeps her compass hidden under her coat, but her body stays aligned with that wax-marked truth: 071° NE. Nolan’s eyes never stop moving—ridge line, rebar spike, patrol poles, the space where an ambush would *want* to live.
