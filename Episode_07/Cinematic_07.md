### 1) SCENE TITLE

THE WRONG QUESTION: Smile Like a Trap

### 2) ESTABLISHING SHOT

The chaotic glow of the Glitch Palace's main floor sweeps back and forth in harsh, strobing waves of cyan and magenta. Here, the air is thick enough to chew—a suffocating mix of stale fryer oil, human sweat, and the sharp, hot-metal tang of overworked transformers. The arcade is a living, breathing beast made of scavenged parts and desperate nostalgia, drowning out the dead world outside with a relentless barrage of 8-bit chimes and pounding synth-bass.

Yet, just outside the Oracle's side chamber, the atmosphere curdles. The transition from the reverent quiet of the shrine to the roaring floor is blocked by a localized pocket of absolute stillness. The neon light hits it, but the "vibe" does not penetrate.

Two figures stand beside the swaying curtain of repurposed cassette ribbons. The arcade surges around them, but they do not move to the rhythm. They are a physical anomaly in the room, their presence acting like a vacuum that sucks the warmth right out of the air.

### 3) THRESHOLD MOMENT

Stepping through the cassette-ribbon curtain feels like stepping onto a frozen lake. The temperature doesn't actually drop, but the sudden, hyper-focused attention of the two strangers makes the hairs on the back of your arms stand up. The noise of the arcade seems to thin out, leaving only the mechanical hum of the nearby cabinets and the sound of your own breathing. The strangers are not looking at the screens. They are looking at you.

### 4) KEY DETAILS

* **The Cassette-Ribbon Curtain:** Swaying gently from the arcade's ventilation draft, providing a flimsy, transparent barrier.
* **The Plain Jackets:** Wind-chapped but aggressively clean, devoid of patches, pins, or the grime of honest scavenging. (Implies Danger)
* **The Clean Hands:** Too smooth and uncalloused for anyone just "passing through" the Frontier.
* **The Etched Metal Tag:** Hooked to the tall one's belt, briefly catching the neon light—a plain, stamped circle with a slash.
* **The Scent of Smoke:** Clinging heavily to their clothes, smelling of ash and burning plastic rather than tobacco.
* **The Back Wall Conduit:** Thick, fossilized veins of old wiring running along the rear of the building, vibrating with a faint carrier tone. (Implies Opportunity)
* **The Attendant's Broom Handle:** Tapping once, sharply, on the floor behind you—a frantic warning to leave.

### 5) PEOPLE IN THE ROOM

The Nostalgist crowd continues its chaotic orbit, completely blind to the wolves in their sanctuary. The Strangers themselves operate with flat, terrifying efficiency. They don't posture like street thugs; they hold the relaxed, perfectly balanced stillness of professional hunters. The tall one attempts to fake casual annoyance, but his companion’s hand hovers inside his coat—a threat meant to be felt, not seen. Behind you, the Attendant has lost all their theatrical bravado. They are genuinely terrified, hissing at you to use the back exits before the trap snaps shut.

### 6) CHARACTER BLOCKS

* **Alex / The Diplomat**
* **What they notice first:** The strangers' absolute failure to blend into the arcade's subculture.
* **What they want:** To aggressively dismantle their cover story using weaponized friendliness.
* **What they fear:** That the silent companion with the hidden hand is going to draw steel before Alex finishes talking.
* **How they position their body:** Immovable but relaxed, leaning in with a warm smile, physically blocking their exit vector like a friendly brick wall.


* **Mila / The Engineer**
* **What they notice first:** The needle-bright carrier tone cutting through the arcade's bass.
* **What they want:** To track that signal to its source before they are forced to flee.
* **What they fear:** That the Analog Order isn't just watching—they are broadcasting a target lock.
* **How they position their body:** Head tilted slightly, eyes closed for half a second, acting as a human antenna to isolate the frequency.


* **Nolan / The Vanguard**
* **What they notice first:** The Analog Order tag flashing on the tall watcher's belt.
* **What they want:** To get the crew out of the kill box immediately, using any door that isn't the front one.
* **What they fear:** The watchers circling around to the alleyway to set up an ambush.
* **How they position their body:** Jaw tight, eyes darting past the watchers to map the service corridors and kitchen hatches behind the arcade cabinets.



### 7) THE RELIC / MACHINE / FOCUS OBJECT

The focus here shifts to the architecture itself—specifically, the **Fossilized Conduit** running along the arcade's back wall. Decades before the Disconnect, these thick metal pipes carried heavy data lines and industrial power. Now, they are supposed to be dead, hollow veins.

But as Mila tunes out the noise of the arcade, she realizes the arcade's chaotic wiring is acting like a massive, crude receiver. The conduit isn't dead. It is singing. The heavy metal pipes are resonating with a frequency that shouldn't exist, vibrating with a cold, electric pulse that draws her attention away from the immediate threat of the scouts.

### 8) THE UNDER-SIGNAL

The static has sharpened from a vague shiver into a needle-bright tone. It no longer feels like a phantom itch; it is a clear, deliberate carrier signal riding underneath the thumping synth-bass. When Alex notices it, the tall watcher sees his eyes shift. In that microsecond, the dynamic changes. The watcher knows that Alex knows. The static ceases to be just an environmental anomaly—it becomes the silent alarm that ends the conversation and forces the hunters to melt back into the shadows.

### 10) PLAYER HOOK + CHOICES

The Analog Order scouts vanish into the crowd. The side chamber behind you feels like a cage. The attendant urges you toward the back.

**So tell me—what happens next?**

* **A) Take the staff corridor.** Nolan takes point, moving the trio through the service and kitchen hatches to exit the building the way locals do, prioritizing a stealthy escape before the Order's attention hardens. *(Stealth/Tactical approach; low risk of immediate confrontation, but sacrifices the chance to track the mysterious signal).*
* **B) Follow the conduit.** Mila ignores the exit and moves deeper into the arcade, using the building's wiring like an antenna to triangulate the source of the carrier tone *now*, before they leave and lose the thread. *(Tech/Investigation approach; high reward for discovering the signal's origin, but high risk of remaining inside a compromised building).*
* **C) Plant a decoy rumor.** Alex steps back into the crowd to plant a rumor with a nearby Nostalgist circle, turning their Oracle visit into harmless "vibe devotion" to muddy the waters while they slip out. *(Social/Diplomacy approach; medium risk, uses the arcade's whisper-network as a shield, but requires spending more time exposed on the main floor).*
