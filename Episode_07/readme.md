# Episode 07 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex goes straight at the watchers with a smile—turning their quiet surveillance into a social problem. He threads through the arcade crowd like he belongs to it, neon flicker striping his face in cyan and magenta. The two strangers by the cassette-ribbon curtain don’t move until he’s close enough to make retreat awkward. “Hey,” Alex says, easy. Warm. Like he’s greeting someone at a mall food court in ‘86. “You look a little… underpatched for the Glitch Palace. First time?” One of them—tall, wind-chapped, hands too clean—tilts his head. “Just passing through.” Alex nods, as if that’s charming. “Passing through is how people get lost. You want a cabinet recommendation or a safe exit?”
