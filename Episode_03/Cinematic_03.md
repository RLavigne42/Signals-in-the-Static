### 1) SCENE TITLE

THE MACHINE ORACLE: Truth, Talent, or Treasure

### 2) ESTABLISHING SHOT

The heavy steel door slams shut, cutting off the bitter wind of the street. Inside, the world goes electric in every way that still works. The Glitch Palace exhales a blast of warm air that smells intensely of hot dust, decades-old carpet, and burnt sugar. The darkness of 2030 is entirely banished here, replaced by the bleeding glow of neon tubes and the flickering phosphor of tired CRT monitors.

Power here isn't just a utility; it's a religion. A restless, constant chorus of coin-chimes, 8-bit arpeggios, and synth stabs ricochet off the cracked tile floors. Cables thick as pythons run along the ceiling, feeding scavenged generator juice into rows of machines that hum like they are dreaming.

The air is thick with the ambient heat of overclocked processors and human bodies. Nostalgists drift between the glowing cabinets, their silhouettes passing through the cyan and magenta light. This isn't just an arcade. It’s a cathedral built from the wreckage of the digital age.

### 3) THRESHOLD MOMENT

Moving away from the frantic energy of the main floor, the trio pushes through a curtain of dangling, repurposed cassette-tape ribbons. They step into a secluded side chamber on the far edge of the noise. The temperature drops a fraction of a degree. The frantic, flashing lights give way to shadows, and the pounding arcade music dulls to a muffled thud.

The dust on the floor here hasn't been danced on; it has been knelt on. Fresh scuff marks form a semi-circle around a hulking shape in the center of the room, leaving a respectful distance.

### 4) KEY DETAILS

* **DDR Pads:** Out on the main floor, worn down to the bare foam by decades of religious stomping. (Implies Lore)
* **The Hand-Painted Halo:** Hovering over a functioning Pac-Man cabinet in the distance.
* **The Denim Tarp:** Stitched together from old jeans and bandanas, draped reverently over a massive arcade cabinet in the center of the side chamber.
* **The Grafted Keyboard:** A yellowed Commodore 64 keyboard physically wired into the front of the cabinet like a priest grafting scripture into an idol. (Implies Opportunity)
* **The Drip-Paint Sign:** Spray-painted on the wall above: *THE MACHINE ORACLE. ASK CLEAN. PAY CLEAN. LEAVE CLEAN.*
* **The Broom Handle:** Held horizontally by the attendant, barring the path to the Oracle's stool. (Implies Danger)

### 5) PEOPLE IN THE ROOM

Out on the floor, the Nostalgists wear patched denim and leather, with dull brass arcade tokens sewn onto their lapels like military medals. But here in the side chamber, there is only the Attendant. Their cheeks are smeared with industrial glitter, and their hair is teased into an impossible, gravity-defying silhouette. They don't drift like the others; they stand perfectly rigid. Their gaze flicks over the trio—fast, practiced, and utterly unfooled by Alex's earlier confidence at the door. They guard the tarp-covered machine with the fierce, unblinking intensity of a zealot.

### 6) CHARACTER BLOCKS

* **Alex / The Diplomat**
* **What they notice first:** The rigid posture of the attendant and the spray-painted rules on the wall.
* **What they want:** To earn a "clean ask" without spending the heavy currency of a physical relic.
* **What they fear:** The bunker map hidden inside his coat burning a metaphorical hole in his pocket.
* **How they position their body:** Loose but anchored, ready to spin a story, treating the attendant as an equal rather than an obstacle.


* **Mila / The Engineer**
* **What they notice first:** The hand-soldered wiring connecting the Commodore 64 keyboard to the cabinet’s guts.
* **What they want:** To pull the denim tarp back and stabilize whatever fragile tech is humming underneath.
* **What they fear:** That the machine is degrading and the Nostalgists' superstitions will prevent her from saving it.
* **How they position their body:** Leaning aggressively forward, fingers twitching, reading the visible circuits by pure instinct.


* **Nolan / The Vanguard**
* **What they notice first:** The auditory anomaly bleeding through the room's acoustics.
* **What they want:** To figure out who holds the real power—and the real tokens—in this room.
* **What they fear:** The strange, sharp noise he hears beneath the music.
* **How they position their body:** Back to the Oracle, head tilted slightly, scanning the shadows and watching the curtain they just walked through.



### 7) THE RELIC / MACHINE / FOCUS OBJECT

The Machine Oracle sits heavily under its stitched denim shroud. It isn't just powered; it feels *latent*. A low, patient vibration hums through the floorboards beneath it, like a CRT warming its ancient bones.

The yellowed keys of the grafted Commodore 64 keyboard are worn smooth, the letters half-erased by thousands of desperate, searching fingers. The attendant enforces the machine's ritual with absolute strictness: *Oracle time ain't free.* You must feed it truth, talent, or treasure. It is treated not as a database, but as a slumbering entity that must be appeased before it will speak.

### 8) THE UNDER-SIGNAL

While the attendant demands their toll, Nolan hears it.

Beneath the heavy bass thump of the arcade floor. Beneath the cascade of coin-chimes. *Static.* It is thin as a whisper and sharp as a razor blade. It isn't coming from a blown speaker or a poorly wired amplifier. It’s coming from somewhere deeper inside the Glitch Palace. It carries a heavy, unnatural pressure, like the drop in barometric air right before a severe thunderstorm. And based on the rigid, sudden stillness of the attendant's shoulders... they hear it too.

### 9) CHARLOTTE RADIO FRAME

"This is Charlotte, broadcasting from the Red Griffin Inn in Oakhaven. The Glitch Palace doesn’t welcome you. It tests you. The old world survives in scraps of song, half-remembered slogans, and doors that only open for the right kind of believer. But when you ask the past to speak, you have to be ready for the voice that answers. Keep your voice steady near machines that remember. And keep one ear on the noise beneath the music."

### 10) PLAYER HOOK + CHOICES

The attendant’s broom handle remains locked across the stool. The static hums beneath the floorboards. The Oracle waits for its tribute.

**So tell me—what happens next?**

* **A) Offer a careful story.** Alex steps up, using diplomacy and old-world lore to convince the attendant they are worthy of a single, clean ask without paying extra. *(Diplomacy/Lore approach; low immediate risk, relies heavily on Alex's charisma to bypass a physical toll).*
* **B) Inspect the Oracle's wiring.** Mila bypasses the attendant entirely, using her engineering skills to stabilize the machine and earn access. *(Hands-on Tech approach; high risk, high reward, risks violently breaking the Nostalgists' sacred rules by touching the idol).*
* **C) Work the room.** Nolan turns his back on the machine, quietly bartering with nearby Nostalgists for Oracle tokens and keeping their relics hidden. *(Social/Barter approach; medium risk, avoids the Oracle's immediate demands while revealing the arcade's hidden power structure).*
