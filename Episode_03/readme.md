# Episode 03 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex goes diplomatic—half confession, half performance. He lifts both hands, palms open in the neon spill. “That line’s from Back to the Future,” he says, voice easy, like he’s letting them in on a secret instead of begging for a door. “And the car… c’mon, you know this—steel gullwings, nuclear dream. The DeLorean.” The shorter bouncer’s mirrored lenses catch the sign’s cyan flicker. He nods once—slow, approving. The taller one leans forward, cassette still spinning on his finger like a tiny planet. “Say it like you mean it,” he growls, but the edge is gone now. It’s ritual, not threat. Alex doesn’t flinch. “DeLorean. Time machine. Bad ideas. Great soundtrack.”
