# Episode 04 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex chooses the cleanest weapon he owns: a story told like a cassette you’ve rewound a hundred times—creased, warm, and true. He steps up to the glitter-cheeked attendant with the broom-handle bar across the stool and nods toward the denim-draped cabinet. The Machine Oracle hums under its tarp, a low, patient vibration—like a CRT warming its bones. “We’re not here to strip-mine your shrine,” Alex says, voice soft enough to fit the room. “We’re here because the old world left breadcrumbs, and someone—someone like you—kept the oven hot.” The attendant’s eyes narrow, measuring him. Around you, the arcade’s noise washes in: coin-chimes, synth pulses, the sweet burn of ozone from overworked transformers.
