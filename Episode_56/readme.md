# Episode 56 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan commits to the MAINT. RETURN — CHAMBER ACCESS crawl. He drops to a knee, slips through the grate, and lets the bunker swallow him into a low, rectangular throat of concrete and sheet metal. The smell changes immediately—less iodine, more dust and old insulation, a faint sweetness like sun-baked vinyl. His elbows scrape, slow and controlled. Every movement is measured, because sound carries weird in places like this—echoes folding back on themselves like warped cassette tape. Behind him, Jessa hovers at the opening, eyes reflecting Nolan’s thin beam. She doesn’t follow—she can’t fit as clean, and she knows it. Instead, she presses two fingers to her lips, then points down the crawl: *go*.
