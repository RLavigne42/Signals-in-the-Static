# Episode 40 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex goes for paper—because paper doesn’t lie the way people do. In the sealed dark, he finds a wall rack by fingertip: plastic sleeves, clipped binders, a metal index box with a latch that snaps open like an old cassette case. The air tastes of dust and dry starch. Somewhere beyond the STACK door, a body hits steel again—BANG—testing the dogs. Nolan plants his shoulder near the wheel, ready to catch a failure with muscle. Mila keeps one palm on the wall, feeling for vibration, for airflow, for the bunker’s old lungs. Alex flips pages by touch.
