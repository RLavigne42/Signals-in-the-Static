# Episode 60 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex doubles down on the bluff—no poetry, no pleading. Just cold procedure, delivered like a stamped order. “PURGE CYCLE—STEP TWO.” His voice cuts through the steel seam and into the corridor, crisp as tearing cassette tape. “VENTING IN TEN SECONDS. STAND CLEAR OF THE DOOR. DO NOT INHALE. DO NOT TOUCH.” Mila feels the words land outside like thrown gravel—small impacts, sudden doubt. Nolan, still cinched behind the torch-bearer, feels the man’s body go rigid with fear. For a heartbeat, the Analog Order isn’t a doctrine. It’s three humans deciding whether faith is worth coughing up blood.
