# Episode 55 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan chooses quiet over speed. He takes Jessa’s pry tool—flat, scarred, honest steel—and slides its edge under the painted-over cap screw on the hinge-side. He doesn’t *twist* so much as *breathe* pressure into it, millimeter by millimeter, until the old paint gives with a soft, dry crack like a cassette case snapping shut. No ringing ping. No betraying chime. He works the cap free and finds the service pin beneath—thick as a pencil, greased once upon a time, now tacky with age. Nolan wraps it in cloth to muffle it, then pulls. The decon door shifts. Heavy. Balanced. Waiting for permission. From deeper in the bunker, a colder draft exhales through the seam—iodine, dust, and something faintly metallic, like pennies left in rain.
