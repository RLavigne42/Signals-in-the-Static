# Episode 52 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan commits to the bypass. On the ladder, he goes statue-still—then moves like a locksmith in a gunfight. One hand clamps the rung to kill vibration; the other fishes out the last of their cloth tape, tears it with his teeth, and presses it gently onto the hair-thin filament. The tape kisses the line and drinks up its tremble. Jessa’s breath hangs above him, tight and silent. Nolan slides two fingers under the taped filament and lifts—slow, slow—until it clears the rung. The striker box doesn’t twitch. No spring snap. No shutter slam. Just the bunker’s dry, patient silence. He eases past, one rung at a time, keeping his shoulder from brushing the concrete. Jessa follows, hands still visible, eyes wide in the shaft-dark.
