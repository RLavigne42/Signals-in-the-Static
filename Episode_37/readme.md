# Episode 37 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan moves *now*. He doesn’t sprint—sprinting is a confession. He slides the trio deeper into the service bay in a low, controlled crawl, shoulders brushing concrete that smells like old paint and dry paper. His palm guides Alex’s sleeve; his boot nudges Mila’s knee when she’s about to knock a crate band. In the dark, he’s not just a scout—he’s a metronome. Behind the sealed panel, the Analog Order’s hunters commit to the lie Nolan wrote for them: two sets of footfalls drift away, chasing the faint tch-tch of tag-chain and the staged scuff line. The heavy one lingers near the seam, listening, patient as a priest. Then Nolan’s wedge whispers again—tick… skitter—and the heavy one shifts. Not away. *Closer.* Close enough that you can hear...
