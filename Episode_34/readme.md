# Episode 34 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex takes point, because the binder told him how to move through a place like this: no white light, no careless footsteps, no heroics. He closes the protocols to a dog-eared diagram and tucks it under his arm like a shield. Mila kills the pinhole beam. The vault swallows you whole—neon-less, moonless—just the taste of dry air and the faint, medicinal tang of old shielding. Nolan’s hand finds the back of Alex’s jacket. Alex’s fingers find the southern rack by memory and touch. Metal shelving: cold. Canister tags: thin, tinny, trembling when the bulkhead takes another THUNK. Floor: unnervingly clean, like the dust itself was ordered to stand down.
