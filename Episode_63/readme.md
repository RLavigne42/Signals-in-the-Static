# Episode 63 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex peels away from the door and its trembling seam of shadow, letting Nolan’s bulk and Mila’s braced stance hold the line. He moves by touch more than sight—palms sliding over cold cinderblock, fingertips catching on flaking stencil paint and the raised edges of old placards. The chamber is a preserved pocket of another century: dust that hasn’t been disturbed in decades, air that tastes like paper, steel, and faint ozone. The bunker’s static is still there—thin, persistent—like a cassette hiss you can’t quite tune out. Alex finds a bulletin board behind a cracked plexi cover. The cover squeals when he lifts it, and the sound feels too loud in a place this sealed.
