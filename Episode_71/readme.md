# Episode 71 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex chooses discipline over force. He turns the handwheel in measured increments—a quarter-turn, then a pause. He leans in close, listening for the bunker’s response the way he’d listen to a warped cassette: not for what you *want* to hear, but for what’s actually there. Whooomp. Air slides through hidden ducts. The corridor’s reflective tape trembles. The inner door’s gap widens another finger-width… and holds. Alex waits. Counts under his breath. Watches the door path like the placard demanded: STAND CLEAR OF DOOR PATH. Another careful quarter-turn. This time the ductwork answers with a sharper sigh—pressure equalizing, not just moving. Somewhere inside the wall, a mechanical indicator clicks from blank to a single stamped word...
