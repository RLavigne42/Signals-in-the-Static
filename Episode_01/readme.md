# Episode 01 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

You steer the trio toward the nearest Glowing 80s Arcade—the Glitch Palace—following the tattered Bunker Map’s inked arrows through cracked overpasses and wind-sanded billboards. The neon glow ahead isn’t just light; it’s a promise. And a trap, if the wrong eyes notice.
