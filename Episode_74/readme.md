# Episode 74 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Mila doesn’t gamble on the jammed dispenser. She makes the call over her shoulder—sharp, practical: “Alex\! I’m bringing *paper*. Make it count.” She tears a strip from what the decon alcove still has: a brittle maintenance log sheet, pre-printed with tiny boxes and faint blue lines. Not a credential, not officially—just *the right kind of thing* in a bunker built by people who trusted rollers and levers more than they trusted humans. She jogs back, boots scuffing dust, cassette deck hugged to her ribs. Behind her, Nolan’s silhouette holds the corridor mouth—smoke crawling low like fog off a dead river, and beyond it the Analog Order’s work-rhythm: THUD… THUD… metal on metal.
