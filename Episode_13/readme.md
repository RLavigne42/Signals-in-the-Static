# Episode 13 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Mila ducks under a patched vinyl awning where the Relic Market’s lantern-light can’t quite reach. Rain ticks faintly on plastic sheeting overhead—soft, patient, like fingers drumming on a cassette case. She unrolls the bunker map against a crate stenciled with faded ARCADE PARTS. The paper’s tired, creased into old decisions. With the new grease pencil, she marks fast and sure: the approximate coordinates, then a bold arrow and a clean annotation: 071° NE — KEEP TO ALLEYS / AVOID OPEN STREETS The mechanical compass clicks as she sets it, needle trembling, then settling with stubborn certainty. No batteries. No prayers. Just north. Alex leans close, shielding the map with his coat like it’s a candle in wind. Nolan watches the market...
