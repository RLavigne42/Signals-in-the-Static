# Episode 35 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan holds up two fingers—*ten seconds*—then melts away from the crawl mouth like a shadow detaching from the wall. In the blackout, his world becomes texture and timing: the cold bite of concrete under his palm, the faint vibration of shelving when the bulkhead takes another THUNK, the whisper-hiss of the vault’s lungs running LOW under Mila’s setting. He doesn’t need light to lie convincingly. He drags a loose chain of canister tags—just enough to make a soft, tinny tch-tch—and lets it trail toward the far end of the shelves, away from the southern rack. Then he scuffs a boot heel in a short, deliberate arc—one false “panic pivot”—and smears a pinch of grit into a line that says *someone ran this way*.
