# Episode 26 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan peels away from the culvert mouth without a word—just a shift of weight, a breath held, then gone into the broken geometry of the concrete ribs. Dust puffs under his heel and dies immediately in the culvert’s cold draft. He climbs where the structure is jagged enough to hide him, hands finding old rebar like guitar strings in the dark. Below, Mila keeps her wire loop steady, listening to the relay’s tight hiss. Alex stays at the plate, lantern hooded, eyes still on the mixed rivets—authentic system, recently tended. *A real key… in a place that expects someone to try it.* Nolan reaches the rib’s crest and flattens himself against the concrete.
