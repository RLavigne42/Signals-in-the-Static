# Episode 31 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Mila follows Alex’s found-protocol to the letter. She turns the rotary selector with two careful clicks until it points to B. The panel answers with a faint internal *hiss*—not air escaping, but air being invited through a valve that hasn’t moved in decades. The bunker’s wall-ticking cycles: tick… tick… then that soft relay click—the “safe” moment Alex promised. Mila’s lips move in silence as she counts. …seventeen… eighteen… nineteen… twenty. She grips the red wheel-handle and turns. For a heartbeat, the needle on the gauge shudders—a quick tremble like a cassette spool catching on warped tape. Mila freezes instantly, honoring the stencil. She waits it out—one breath, then another—until the needle settles back into a steady line.
