# Episode 21 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan slips away from the canopy’s shade like a rumor—low, quiet, and inevitable. The rest stop basin is a dead stage: cracked asphalt, skeletal billboard, vending machines gutted like arcade cabinets after a riot. Wind drags grit across the ground with a sound like a tape head chewing ribbon. The clack… clack… clack keeps time somewhere ahead—steady, mechanical, patient. He moves along the line of collapsed concrete, using sun-bleached shadows and broken rebar as cover. Close to the billboard frame, he finds the source. Not a person. A device.
