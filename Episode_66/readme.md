# Episode 66 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex takes the QZ-3 cassette from Mila like it’s a holy thing that might shatter under breath. He doesn’t pocket it. He cradles it—thumb over the label—then turns away from the red-striped cabinet and begins a fast, methodical sweep of the chamber. His eyes don’t hunt randomly. They hunt like a man who’s spent years reading the bones of old rooms: where people *stood*, where they *learned*, where they *repeated procedures until they could do them half-asleep in gas masks*. The bunker gives him clues in texture and residue. A waist-high desk with a shallow groove worn into its edge—someone leaned there for hours. A wall where the paint is cleaner in a rectangle—something used to hang there. A scatter of thumbtack holes in a precise grid:...
