# Episode 75 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex takes the RETRY like a gift with teeth. One more chance before the bunker decides you’re noise. Mila keeps her hand near the paper slot, feeling the rollers’ pulse through the metal. Nolan’s voice carries from the smoky corridor mouth—tight, low: “They’re moving something heavy.” Alex wipes dust off the placard again, like the right smear might reveal the right truth: …ENTER AUTH DATE / SHIFT ID… The worn keys—1, 9, 8, 0—still tell him the bunker’s favorite story, but the half-retracted bolt told another: *close isn’t correct.* So he changes the *date*, not the identity.
