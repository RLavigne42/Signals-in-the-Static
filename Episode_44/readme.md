This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Mila commits to the inner cycle—vent → hold—the way you’d tune a radio: tiny adjustments, patient listening, no wasted motion. She nudges the three-position control to VENT. The corridor answers with a soft, controlled exhale through hidden ducting. The air pressure in P‑2 eases—just enough that the inner door won’t *clap* when it breaks its seal. She waits, eyes half-lidded, palm on the steel, feeling for that moment when the door stops pushing back. Behind you, smoke is no longer hypothetical. It’s a thin, oily ribbon curling under the false panel and tasting the corridor like a tongue. Still not a flood—but close enough to sting the back of your throat.
