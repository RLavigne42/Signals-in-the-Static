# Episode 32 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex pulls the VAULT PROTOCOLS (ANALOG) binder from its plastic sleeve with the care of a man lifting a holy text off an altar. The sleeve crackles—dry, sharp—too loud in this organized dark. He opens to the first tabbed section. The pages are thick, slightly waxed, meant to survive damp, time, and human panic. A bold header stares back in block print: IMMEDIATE ENTRY PROCEDURE — DO NOT USE WHITE LIGHT. 1) CONFIRM SEAL INTEGRITY. 2) VERIFY AIR HANDLING (MANUAL). 3) INVENTORY CANISTERS WITHOUT MOVEMENT. 4) IF BREACH SUSPECTED: INITIATE “BLACKOUT HOLD.” Alex’s eyes flick to a margin note—handwritten, older ink: “If you hear *tools* on the outer slab, you’re already late.”
