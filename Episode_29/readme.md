# Episode 29 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan takes the inner handle with both hands like it’s the neck of a stubborn machine—something you don’t *fight*, you *persuade*. He looks at Alex once. Alex answers with a silent count, fingers barely moving in the lantern’s pinhole glow. One… two… three… Outside, a shape blocks the culvert’s grey mouth. A voice—close enough now to carry consonants—murmurs a hard, practiced phrase, and another answers. Not scavengers. Not lost. Four… five… Nolan leans his weight in, steady, controlled. The slab grinds a fraction—stone on stone, old grease turning to grit. Mila holds her breath so the sound of it doesn’t ride on her exhale. Six… seven… eight…
