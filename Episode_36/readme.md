# Episode 36 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan goes still—so still the bunker’s dry air seems to forget him. In the crawlspace dark, he turns his head a fraction at a time, letting sound paint the room. Behind the sealed panel: boots. At least three—one heavier, two lighter. Breath through cloth. A pry bar dragged with ritual patience. The Analog Order doesn’t rush; they *purify*. Then Nolan’s little wedge does what it was born to do: tick… skitter—a tiny betrayal on the other side of the wall. A voice, muffled and pleased: “There. Movement.” Metal scrapes. A flashlight *tries* to exist—dies into uselessness, just a faint click and a curse. The Disconnect still owns the world. Nolan counts again. The heavy one gives orders.
