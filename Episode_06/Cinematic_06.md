### 1) SCENE TITLE

OBSERVATION: The Plainclothes Threat

### 2) ESTABLISHING SHOT

The suffocating heat inside the Glitch Palace's side chamber suddenly breaks. The moment Alex finishes his speech—tapping his chest and speaking of *the keeping*—the ambient tension in the arcade dissipates. The oppressive neon glare feels less like an interrogation spotlight and more like the warm, stained-glass light of a sanctuary.

Out on the main floor, the heavy, rhythmic synth-bass settles back into a comfortable, driving groove. The restless murmurs of the Nostalgists are swallowed once again by the chaotic symphony of 8-bit explosions, coin-chimes, and the mechanical clatter of joysticks. The air still smells of stale sweat and ozone, but the sharp, metallic tang of suspicion has faded.

The crowd of patched leather and denim resumes its orbit around the glowing cabinets. A girl in fingerless gloves takes a long sip from a dented metal canteen; a player at a Galaga cabinet leans back, satisfied. The trio has been officially folded into the arcade’s ecosystem. They are no longer outsiders. They are devotees.

### 3) THRESHOLD MOMENT

The transition from the Oracle's quiet chamber back toward the sensory overload of the main floor should be a relief, but the temperature suddenly feels wrong. At the threshold of the side room, where the dangling curtain of repurposed cassette tape ribbons sways in the arcade's ventilation draft, the visual noise of the crowd is interrupted.

Two figures stand just beyond the ribbons. They aren't playing. They aren't cheering. In a room defined by its chaotic, colorful excess, their absolute stillness creates a localized vacuum of sound and light.

### 4) KEY DETAILS

* **The Folded Dot-Matrix Strip:** Tucked safely away in Alex's pocket, holding the coordinates to the *Fused Interchange*. (Implies Opportunity)
* **The Attendant's Broom Handle:** Now held vertically like a shepherd’s staff, a sign of protective authority over the trio.
* **The Galaga Cabinet:** Flashing its attract-mode lights nearby, offering a perfect physical blind spot to slip behind.
* **The Cassette-Ribbon Curtain:** Rustling softly, acting as a flimsy, transparent barrier between the trio and the watchers.
* **The Strangers' Jackets:** Too plain. Too clean. Unscuffed fabric with zero patches, zero bandanas, and no arcade tokens. (Implies Danger)

### 5) PEOPLE IN THE ROOM

The Nostalgists are entirely blind to the threat in their midst, lost in the digital glow of their high scores. The Attendant, however, has become an unexpected ally; they lean in close, their glitter-streaked face tight with real fear, whispering the warning not to let the strangers follow them out.

But it’s the Strangers themselves who command the room's gravity. They move with the predatory, calculating stillness of Analog Order scouts. They do not wander. Their eyes do not track the flashing screens. Their gaze punches straight through the cassette ribbons and locks onto the trio. It is pure, unfiltered *Observation*—the clinical targeting that always precedes a fire.

### 6) CHARACTER BLOCKS

* **Alex / The Diplomat**
* **What they notice first:** The sudden, jarring lack of "vibe" radiating from the two watchers.
* **What they want:** To maintain his newly minted cover as a harmless analog devotee.
* **What they fear:** That the watchers have already matched his face to an Analog Order bounty board.
* **How they position their body:** Relaxed, posture open, holding the performance of a satisfied worshipper just leaving a shrine.


* **Mila / The Engineer**
* **What they notice first:** The lingering, unnatural carrier tone still humming in her teeth.
* **What they want:** To track the signal bleeding into the district before they are forced to flee the building.
* **What they fear:** That the plainclothes strangers are carrying the tech emitting that static.
* **How they position their body:** Perfectly still, head tilted slightly like she’s listening to a ghost station, eyes scanning the thick power cables running overhead.


* **Nolan / The Vanguard**
* **What they notice first:** The tactical layout of the room and the clean sightlines of the strangers.
* **What they want:** To control the exit vector and prevent the trio from being followed into the dark streets.
* **What they fear:** An ambush waiting outside the heavy steel doors of the Glitch Palace.
* **How they position their body:** Drifting a smooth step to the left, seamlessly putting his own silhouette between the watchers and the rest of his crew.



### 7) THE RELIC / MACHINE / FOCUS OBJECT

The focus is no longer a single arcade cabinet, but the Glitch Palace's entire **Ambient Wiring Array**. Thick bundles of copper wire, coax cables, and patched power lines run along the ceiling like exposed veins, feeding the neon and the CRTs.

To Mila, this chaotic web of scavenged infrastructure isn't just delivering power; it's acting as a massive, crude antenna. The wiring is vibrating with the ghost frequency she felt in the Oracle's chamber. It is a massive, unintentional receiver for a signal that shouldn't exist in a dead world.

### 8) THE UNDER-SIGNAL

The static has evolved. It is no longer the sharp, violent *snap* of the Oracle's dot-matrix printer. It has become a sustained, low-frequency shiver in the air—a carrier tone bleeding into the entire district.

It hums underneath the arcade's synth music, an invisible pressure that makes the hairs on the back of the neck stand up. It feels like the air before a lightning strike. Outside these walls, the world is supposed to be dust and silence. But the static proves that something out there in the dark is powered up, broadcasting, and wide awake.

### 9) CHARLOTTE RADIO FRAME

"This is Charlotte, broadcasting from the Red Griffin Inn in Oakhaven. Thirty years after the Disconnect, we’re all just trying to keep what’s left from getting erased. But memory is a dangerous thing to wake up, especially in a crowded room. When you feel eyes on your back, don’t sprint. Don’t freeze. Move like you’ve got nothing to hide—and everything to lose. Keep one ear on the noise beneath the music."

### 10) PLAYER HOOK + CHOICES

The plainclothes strangers stand at the curtain. Nolan's body blocks their direct line of sight. Mila listens to the unnatural hum in the wires. The exit feels a million miles away.

**So tell me—what happens next?**

* **A) Approach with a smile.** Alex walks straight up to the observers, welcoming them with easy diplomacy and "vibe-talk" to force them to improvise a cover story while he reads their tells. *(Diplomacy/Social approach; low immediate physical risk, turns the spotlight back on the watchers, but requires perfect execution to avoid blowing their own cover).*
* **B) Use the wiring as an antenna.** Mila turns her focus to the arcade's power cables, following the faint carrier shiver toward its strongest point to triangulate the source without stepping outside yet. *(Tech/Investigation approach; high reward for locating the mysterious signal, but risks ignoring the physical threat standing at the door).*
* **C) Shadow the observers.** Nolan melts into the moving bodies of the arcade floor, shadowing the watchers from inside to get close enough to overhear who they answer to before peeling away. *(Stealth/Tactical approach; medium risk, gains vital intel on the enemy faction, but leaves Alex and Mila exposed for a few crucial seconds).*
