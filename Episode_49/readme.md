# Episode 49 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan commits to the crawl. He inches forward on forearms and boot soles, belly scraping cold metal, the air tasting of powdered insulation and old ozone. The darkness ahead isn’t empty—there’s that scritch… scritch again, too steady to be vermin. Human breath, controlled and close. He stops, lets the bunker’s tick… tick… tick cover him, and listens for the smallest tells: cloth, buckles, the faint clink of a tool. Nothing jingles. That’s what scares him. Nolan slides one palm to his knife and advances another yard. Then another—slow enough that even dust doesn’t dare rise. A shape resolves: a crouched figure in the spine, back turned, working at a panel seam with something flat and metal. Not chanting. Not praying. Just… *opening*.
