# Episode 10 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex keeps the mixtape visible—like a badge, like a harmless trophy—then guides the trio deeper into the Glitch Palace with the easy confidence of someone “just looking for a listening corner.” The boombox circle parts for him, pleased with itself. A few Nostalgists call after you—half blessing, half warning—while the neon sign above the cabinets stutters and throws blue-pink bands across the floor like scanlines. Near the back wall, the air changes. Less popcorn. More dust and old ozone. The carrier tone Mila’s been hearing—needle-thin, persistent—tightens in her skull as you pass a run of metal conduit. The sound doesn’t get louder so much as *more certain*, like the building itself is acting as a crude antenna.
