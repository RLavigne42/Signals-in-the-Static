# Episode 65 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Mila commits to the red-stripe cabinet. She crosses the chamber in three quick, quiet steps, boots whispering through dust that hasn’t known a footprint in years. Her fingers find the latch—old military hardware, overbuilt, stubborn. She doesn’t yank. She listens with her hands, feeling where the tension lives. Outside the blast door, the Analog Order shifts—weight on heels, a low murmur, then the scrape of something dragged into position. Mila slides a flat tool from her kit, works the seam, and the cabinet gives a reluctant metallic sigh—like a cassette deck door opening after a decade in storage. Inside: a foam cradle, brittle at the edges… and a cassette in a clear sleeve.
