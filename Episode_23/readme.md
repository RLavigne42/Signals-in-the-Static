# Episode 23 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Mila takes the lead into the shadowed dip, drawn by the hollow thrum like it’s a bassline under the world. She kneels where the concrete ribs converge, sets her compass housing on a flat slab, and threads a length of scavenged wire through its cracked hinge—an improvised loop antenna. Alex holds the map half-open, shielding it from the wind; Nolan stays a few paces back, eyes on the ridge line, counting silence. Mila closes her eyes and listens—not with ears, but with timing. She taps the compass case with a screwdriver in a steady pattern, then pauses, then taps again, matching the distant clack… clack… clack from the basin like a metronome. The air answers with a faint tightening of static, a pressure you feel in your teeth.
