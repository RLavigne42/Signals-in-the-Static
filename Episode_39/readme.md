# Episode 39 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex breaks from the junction toward the STACK door, guided by bunker-instinct and the half-remembered grammar of old government facilities: *storage is never just storage—storage is a maze with rules.* Nolan shadows him like a second silhouette, one hand hovering near Alex’s shoulder to steady him if he stumbles. Mila stays a pace behind, grime-black fingers flexing after the damper fight, her coil giving off a faint, nervous static like a cassette hiss you can’t quite mute. Behind you, the pry bar’s rhythm accelerates—SCRK—SCRK—SCRK—and the heavy one’s voice gets closer to anger than prayer. Alex finds the door by touch: raised letters—S T A C K—and a wheel-lock the size of a dinner plate. The metal is cold enough to bite.
