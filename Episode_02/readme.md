# Episode 02 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex steps out first, letting the neon wash over his battered jacket like stage light. He doesn’t reach for the map or the laptop—nothing that screams relic. He just meets the bouncer’s mirrored shades with the calm of someone who’s spent years living in other people’s decades. The taller Nostalgist cracks his knuckles once. “State. Your. Vibe.” Alex takes a breath that tastes like dust and ozone, and drops his voice into a practiced, friendly cadence. “If the world ended in the eighties, then we’re all still on side B. You don’t skip tracks—you flip the tape.” For a moment, the only sound is the flicker-buzz of the GLI_TCH sign and the distant thump of synth bass behind the door.
