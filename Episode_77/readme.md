# Episode 77 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Inside the bunker’s vestibule, you sealed the hatch hard—wheel latched, gasket biting—while the Analog Order’s THUD-THUD faded into a muffled, steel-thick rage on the other side. The air turned cold and dry, tasting of paper and vinyl. In front of you: an ARCHIVE / GERMINATION door and a mechanical card carousel labeled with logs—SEED LOT INDEX, WATER PURITY, VIABILITY TESTS, and a contingency binder marked “BLACKOUT YEARS.” You ask for a clue—something actionable, right now, without handing you the whole bunker’s soul in one breath.
