# Episode 50 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan chooses trust—but verify. In the maintenance crawl, there’s no room for heroics—only leverage and breath control. Nolan keeps his forearm across the stranger’s collar line, knife angled where it can *warn* without having to *work*. His other hand pats fast: waistband, boot, jacket seam. He finds a slim pry tool, a roll of cloth tape, and a small brass token on a cord—worn smooth by fingers. No firearm. No obvious Order-branding. “Name,” Nolan whispers. “Faction. How’d you get in the spine.” The stranger swallows. In the tight dark, that sound is loud as a cassette click. “Jessa. Tech Nomad. Came in through an exterior vent stack—collapsed access. I heard your door cycling and figured you’d found the chamber before they did.”
