# Episode 33 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Mila slides to the squat crank-and-needle controller, keeping her shoulders tight to the shelving so the pinhole beam doesn’t wander. The device is all analog stubbornness: a glass-faced meter, a knurled selector, and a crank that smells faintly of old oil and ozone. She braces two fingers on the selector and turns it toward AIR / LOW. The bunker answers. Not with light—thank whatever engineer wrote those protocols—but with a soft internal relay-click, then a long, controlled hiss as dampers shift somewhere deep in the walls. The needle on the meter trembles, climbs a hair, then steadies in the green band labeled STABLE. Mila leans close, listening with her whole skull. No alarm chatter. No sudden lockout.
