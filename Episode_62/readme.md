# Episode 62 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Mila commits to the hinge-side. She wedges her shoulder into the cold steel, fingers hunting by feel in the narrow gap where the old engineers hid their redundancies. Her nails scrape paint. She finds it—an oblong housing with a recessed lever, stiff with decades of dust and dried grease. Outside, the corridor is all lantern-flare and cudgel-shadows. Nolan grunts through pain as he keeps bodies from the seam. Alex’s voice is hoarse now, the authority fraying at the edges. Mila sets her jaw and throws her weight into the lever. It doesn’t move. Not at first. Then it gives with a sound like a snapped vinyl record—KRAK—followed by a deep, satisfying CHUNK as a dog-bolt slides home somewhere inside the doorframe.
