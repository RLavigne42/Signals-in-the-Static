### 1) SCENE TITLE

STATIC ON THE LINE: The Boombox Altar

### 2) ESTABLISHING SHOT

The main floor of the Glitch Palace is a sensory assault designed to make you forget the ruins outside. Cyan and magenta light pulses in time with a relentless, thumping 8-bit soundtrack. The air is stifling, smelling of ozone, heated plastic, and the unwashed leather of a hundred Nostalgist jackets. It is an ecosystem of manufactured joy, desperate and loud.

But within that chaos, there are distinct pockets of gravity. Near the back of the room, far from the entrance where the Analog Order scouts are prowling, a dense circle of devotees has formed. They are laughing, trading tokens, and passing around a dented metal canteen. By stepping into this circle, the ambient temperature seems to rise with shared body heat. The harsh glare of the arcade cabinets is blocked by their bodies, creating a warm, insulated shelter in the middle of a warzone.

### 3) THRESHOLD MOMENT

Moving from the exposed aisle into the center of the Nostalgist circle feels like slipping under the surface of rushing water. The direct, predatory sightlines of the scouts are broken. The hostile observation is immediately replaced by the loud, welcoming curiosity of the devotees. The transition is marked by the distinct sound of cassette tape unspooling and the clack of heavy plastic buttons being pressed.

### 4) KEY DETAILS

* **The Cassette Scarves:** Worn by the Nostalgists, shimmering like black water in the neon light.
* **The Dark Glass Screens:** The unpowered sides of the arcade cabinets, acting as perfect mirrors to watch the room behind you. (Implies Opportunity)
* **The Hand-Painted Boombox:** Covered in band stickers and sitting on a milk crate like a religious centerpiece.
* **The Dented Canteen:** Passed around the circle, smelling faintly of distilled alcohol and rust.
* **The STAFF ONLY Door:** A heavy metal door painted to blend into the wall, its hinges rattling slightly as a draft (or a person) pushes against it from the other side. (Implies Danger)
* **The Metal Conduit:** Thick, fossilized pipes running along the ceiling above the door, vibrating with a cold, electric pulse.

### 5) PEOPLE IN THE ROOM

The Nostalgists are an unwitting human shield. They are desperate for a good story, leaning in close when Alex speaks, ready to adopt his lie as their new truth. When they laugh and chant *"follow the old rail,"* they are unwittingly constructing a wall of noise that the Analog Order cannot breach without causing a riot. Out in the periphery, the Scouts have been neutralized by the crowd's density. They are forced to become passive watchers again, their predatory advance blunted by the sheer volume of witnesses.

### 6) CHARACTER BLOCKS

* **Alex / The Diplomat**
* **What they notice first:** The social hunger of the Nostalgist circle.
* **What they want:** To completely overwrite his interaction with the Oracle by planting a harmless, infectious rumor.
* **What they fear:** That the scouts will realize what he's doing and push through the crowd to grab him.
* **How they position their body:** Dead center of the circle, palms open, smiling easily, projecting the entirely unthreatening posture of a late-night talk show host.


* **Mila / The Engineer**
* **What they notice first:** The metal conduit running directly over the Staff Only door.
* **What they want:** To press her hands against the pipe and physically track the impossible radio frequency.
* **What they fear:** That walking out the door means leaving the biggest technological mystery of the night behind.
* **How they position their body:** Edging toward the back wall, eyes tracking the thick wiring, ignoring the crowd completely.


* **Nolan / The Vanguard**
* **What they notice first:** The dark, reflective glass of the nearby Galaga cabinet.
* **What they want:** To push the STAFF ONLY door open and drag his team into the service corridors before the scouts regroup.
* **What they fear:** That the rattle of the staff door means someone is already waiting for them in the alleyway.
* **How they position their body:** Back to the entrance, watching the reflections, calculating the exact distance between Alex and the exit hatch.



### 7) THE RELIC / MACHINE / FOCUS OBJECT

The Boombox Altar sits on a scavenged milk crate. It is an old, heavy, dual-cassette deck from the late 80s, entirely hand-painted with dripping neon geometric shapes. It is powered by a jury-rigged motorcycle battery.

It isn't playing music; it is simply powered on, a tape pausing on Play. The Nostalgists treat it as a symbol of 'the keeping'—the act of preserving human voices. By anchoring his lie to this machine, Alex legitimizes his story. He isn't a data-thief; he's a pilgrim who came to worship at the same altar they do.

### 8) THE UNDER-SIGNAL

While Alex weaves his social camouflage, the true threat in the room shifts from the scouts back to the signal. The carrier tone is growing more insistent. It sounds like a radio trying to clear its throat—a physical, vibrating hum that lives inside the fossilized metal conduit running above the staff door. It is needle-bright, piercing right through the boombox's static and the arcade's bass. It is a breadcrumb trail made of sound, pulling Mila toward the back of the building.

### 9) CHARLOTTE RADIO FRAME

"This is Charlotte, broadcasting from the Red Griffin Inn. Out here in 2030, after the Disconnect taught us what silence costs, we learn to listen for what people don’t say, and to fear the stories that spread faster than footsteps. In a crowded room, the safest disguise isn’t a mask. It’s a story everyone wants to repeat. But stories can’t stop a signal. Keep one ear on the noise beneath the music."

### 10) PLAYER HOOK + CHOICES

Alex's lie has taken root. The crowd is laughing. Nolan stares at the rattling staff door. Mila stares at the humming conduit above it. The window to move is closing.

**So tell me—what happens next?**

* **A) Take the staff-only door.** Nolan guides the trio away from the crowd and through the service corridors, moving fast and quiet to avoid the scouts' line of sight. *(Stealth/Tactical approach; lowers Heat, secures an exit, but risks whatever is waiting in the dark hallways).*
* **B) Trace the carrier tone.** Mila presses to the back wall and tracks the signal through the conduit, triangulating the source inside the building before they leave. *(Tech/Investigation approach; high reward for finding the signal, but leaves the trio dangerously lingering near the exit).*
* **C) Seal the decoy.** Alex trades a scavenged item for a cheap, harmless mixtape right there, making the story undeniable before slipping out with the crowd. *(Social/Barter approach; fully neutralizes the Analog Order's suspicion, but burns resources and delays their escape).* ***
