# Episode 08 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex does it again—weaponizes warmth. He slides back toward a knot of Nostalgists near a battered boombox altar: denim vests, neon laces, a scarf made from cassette tape spools. Their laughter is loud enough to drown suspicion—perfect cover. Alex lifts his hands, palms out. “Quick public service announcement,” he says, grinning like he’s about to tell a joke from a late-night rerun. “If anyone hears a rumor that I was back there trying to *pull secrets* from the Oracle—nah.” A few faces tilt toward him. Hooked. “I asked it something small,” Alex continues, voice earnest, almost reverent. “Like… why we keep the lights on when the world wants them off. And it spit out the most Nostalgist answer imaginable: *follow the old rail.*”
