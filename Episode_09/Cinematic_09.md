### 1) SCENE TITLE

THE DECOY TAPE: Friday Night Drive

### 2) ESTABLISHING SHOT

Deep inside the Glitch Palace, the air is thick enough to chew. The dense pocket of the Nostalgist circle forms a physical and acoustic fortress against the rest of the arcade. Dust dances in the air, caught in the strobing crossfire of cyan and magenta CRT monitors, glittering like particles that have forgotten they are supposed to settle.

A battered boombox altar sits on a milk crate in the center, thumping out a muffled, heavy bassline that vibrates up through the cracked floor tiles. The smell of old denim, sweat, and hot electronics is overpowering, but it creates a warm, insulated pocket of safety. Outside this circle, the arcade is a dangerous, exposed grid, but in here, the neon light feels like a sanctuary.

### 3) THRESHOLD MOMENT

The tension breaks the moment the plastic hinge of the blank cassette case snaps shut in the kid's hand. It is a microscopic sound, but it seals the transaction with absolute finality. The energy of the circle immediately shifts from curious observation to joyous inclusion. Someone slaps Alex on the shoulder. The crowd cheers. The rumor hardens into fact, and the trio is officially bathed in the protective, unquestioning warmth of the local faithful.

### 4) KEY DETAILS

* **The "Friday Night Drive" Cassette:** Hand-lettered in thick, unapologetic marker: *1987 (RECORDING) - DO NOT DUB*. (Implies Lore/Opportunity)
* **The Dot-Matrix Scrap:** Traded by Alex as a prop, completely useless for bunker hunting, but treated like gold by the Nostalgists.
* **The Dark Glass Reflections:** The unpowered arcade screens reflecting the main entrance, where a single, plainclothes shoulder is visible. (Implies Danger)
* **The STAFF ONLY Door:** Breathing a steady draft of fryer oil and damp concrete, the metal rattling softly on its hinges.
* **The Conduit Pipes:** Snaking along the back wall, vibrating in time with the flickering neon sign outside.

### 5) PEOPLE IN THE ROOM

The Nostalgist Crowd acts as an unwitting, impenetrable shield of denim and leather. They own Alex now, fully buying into his tape-hunter persona. The Kid with the shaved sidecut holds the traded blank tape like it’s a religious artifact. Across the room, isolated in the periphery, the Analog Order Scout is visibly frustrated, their profile shifting as they try to reacquire their target through a sea of hostile, loyal locals. But it’s the Attendant who cuts through the celebration. Catching the trio's eyes through the crowd, their look is a match struck in the dark: *Go. Now.*

### 6) CHARACTER BLOCKS

* **Alex / The Diplomat**
* **What they notice first:** The kid's absolute reverence for the traded dot-matrix paper.
* **What they want:** To cement his cover story so deeply that the scouts have nothing to chase but a ghost.
* **What they fear:** That the crowd will demand he play the tape right here, trapping them in the circle.
* **How they position their body:** Relaxed, holding the newly acquired cassette close to his chest like a passport, not hiding it, but not flaunting it either.


* **Mila / The Engineer**
* **What they notice first:** The way the needle-bright static surges every time the arcade's main sign flickers.
* **What they want:** To press her hands against the back wall and trace the carrier tone to its physical anchor.
* **What they fear:** That Nolan will drag them out the door before she can lock the final bearing of the signal.
* **How they position their body:** Stiff, lips barely moving as she whispers, her eyes tracing the ceiling pipes like she’s reading a map only she can see.


* **Nolan / The Vanguard**
* **What they notice first:** The plainclothes shoulder visible in the reflection of the Galaga cabinet.
* **What they want:** To shove through the STAFF ONLY door and put three solid walls between his crew and the watchers.
* **What they fear:** That Alex and Mila are getting too comfortable playing games in a kill-box.
* **How they position their body:** Head forward, not looking at Mila as he speaks, his weight shifted entirely onto his back foot, primed to sprint.



### 7) THE RELIC / MACHINE / FOCUS OBJECT

The focus object is the *Friday Night Drive* cassette tape. It is warped plastic and magnetic ribbon, scuffed from decades of being carried in inside pockets and gloveboxes.

But in the Disconnected Frontier, it is far more than a recording. It is a physical alibi. The thick marker ink on the J-card is proof of the old world's insistence on preserving its own voice. To Alex, it isn't music; it is a shield. As long as he holds this tape, the arcade's whisper-network will protect him as one of their own.

### 8) THE UNDER-SIGNAL

Mila realizes the static isn't just an ambient frequency—it's parasitic. The needle-bright carrier tone is thin as fishing line, but it pulses in exact synchronization with the flickering neon marquee outside. The signal is physically riding the building's scavenged metal grid. It is using the Glitch Palace's own power lines as an antenna. It hums strongest near the back wall, pulling at Mila's instincts, promising that whoever is broadcasting it is tapped directly into the district's dead veins.

### 9) CHARLOTTE RADIO FRAME

"This is Charlotte, at the Red Griffin Inn in Oakhaven. In a world where the grid went dark and never came back, we learned to measure danger the old way: by the weight of eyes on your back, and the way a room’s noise can turn into cover. Remember what the old-timers used to say about crowded rooms: the safest path isn’t always the fastest one, but it’s the one that looks like it was meant for you. Keep one ear on the noise beneath the music."

### 10) PLAYER HOOK + CHOICES

The boombox thumps. The scout searches the crowd. The staff door rattles in its frame.

**So tell me—what happens next?**

* **A) Take the staff-only door.** Nolan takes point through the STAFF ONLY door and guides the trio into the service corridors, moving fast and quiet to keep them completely off the main exit’s sightline. *(Stealth/Tactical approach; low immediate risk, prioritizes a clean escape but leaves the mystery of the signal unsolved).*
* **B) Trace the carrier tone.** Mila moves along the back wall with her palm brushing the conduit and metal frames, tracking where the carrier tone peaks to lock the final northeast bearing before they slip away. *(Tech/Investigation approach; high reward for isolating the signal, but high risk of remaining in the arcade while the scouts actively search for them).*
* **C) Ask for a listening corner.** Alex asks the boombox circle for a "listening corner" to test the traded tape, using the playback as a natural, unthreatening reason to drift toward the back rooms and the exits. *(Social/Diplomacy approach; medium risk, perfectly maintains their cover story, but burns precious time and leaves them exposed to the crowd).*
