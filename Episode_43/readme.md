# Episode 43 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

She slips through the finger-width gap behind the manifest rack, shoulders turning sideways, boots finding the slick grit of old concrete. One hand stays on the recessed EQ handwheel like a safecracker’s dial; the other hovers near the seam, feeling for the slightest pushback. Alex follows, keeping the false panel from rattling, while Nolan brings up the rear—quiet as cassette tape sliding through a deck. Behind them, the STACK door answers with a brutal new note: not the seal failing, but the frame beginning to complain—wood splintering, metal groaning.
