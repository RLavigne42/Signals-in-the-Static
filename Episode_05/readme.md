# Episode 05 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex pays clean—exactly what he promised. He doesn’t reach for the map. He doesn’t try to bargain with metal. He looks the attendant in the eye, under the stitched denim tarp and the Oracle’s sickly green glow, and gives them a truth with weight. “Before the Disconnect,” he says, voice low enough that the main floor’s synth thump can’t steal it, “I cataloged things nobody thought mattered. Commercial jingles. Home-recorded mixtapes. Local news broadcasts. The tiny stuff people laughed at—until the day the world went quiet and that tiny stuff became the only way to remember a voice.” He swallows. The cassette-ribbon curtain behind you whispers as someone passes.
