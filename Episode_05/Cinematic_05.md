### 1) SCENE TITLE

THE WHISPER NETWORK: Bearing Zero-Seven-One

### 2) ESTABLISHING SHOT

The side chamber of the Glitch Palace feels suddenly smaller, the walls pressing in thick with the smell of scorched paper and overworked copper wiring. The sickly green phosphor of the Machine Oracle casts long, bruised shadows over the stitched denim tarp. Outside the room, the relentless pounding of the arcade's synth-bass continues, but its texture has changed.

The mindless, chaotic joy of the Nostalgists is curdling into something focused and sharp. A low, restless murmur ripples underneath the 8-bit chimes, like a barometric pressure drop before a severe storm. The whisper network is awake. Word is moving from cabinet to cabinet, traveling along the scavenged power cables: *Outsiders are in the Oracle's chamber. They made it spit paper.* Inside the dim room, the air feels heavy with the weight of the old world. The frantic energy of the arcade cannot penetrate the quiet pocket of analog mourning that Alex has just created.

### 3) THRESHOLD MOMENT

The standoff at the altar breaks, not with violence, but with grief. When Alex speaks of his sister and the cassette labels—*SIDE A, SIDE B*—the temperature of the room seems to settle. The attendant’s rigid, gatekeeping posture collapses inward. The industrial glitter on their cheeks catches the green CRT glow, looking less like a costume of defiance and more like the dried, metallic tracks of old tears. The broom handle lowers completely. The toll has been paid in full.

### 4) KEY DETAILS

* **The Creased J-Card:** Held out by Alex, a fragile piece of cardboard that holds more currency than scavenged copper. (Implies Lore)
* **The Fresh Tractor-Feed Paper:** Threaded into the dot-matrix printer by the attendant’s reverent hands. (Implies Opportunity)
* **The Cassette-Ribbon Curtain:** Rustling in the doorway as the crowd outside begins to gather and pass by too slowly. (Implies Danger)
* **The Stamped Coordinates:** Clean, unburned text reading: *THE GLASS = FUSED INTERCHANGE. SUNDOWN RIDGE: FOLLOW THE OLD RAIL. BEARING MARK: 071 (NE).* ### 5) PEOPLE IN THE ROOM
The Attendant has shifted from a fierce zealot to a weary projectionist. Their movements are practiced and gentle as they feed the paper into the machine, warning Alex with a soft, genuine dread: *“Don’t make it hungry.”* Out on the floor, the Crowd is transforming into a mob. The Nostalgists are no longer drifting; their body language is pivoting toward the side chamber. The arcade tokens sewn to their jackets clink softly as they shift their weight, waiting to see who emerges from behind the cassette-tape curtain. Attention is the heaviest currency in the Disconnected Frontier, and the trio has just bankrupted the room.

### 6) CHARACTER BLOCKS

* **Alex / The Diplomat**
* **What they notice first:** The profound, human sadness breaking through the attendant's harsh facade.
* **What they want:** To honor the emotional transaction and get the final piece of the map's puzzle safely.
* **What they fear:** That the rising murmur outside will break the fragile peace he just brokered.
* **How they position their body:** Leaning in close, voice dropped to a confessional whisper, holding the J-card like a shared talisman.


* **Mila / The Engineer**
* **What they notice first:** The unnatural, radio-sour shiver of static crawling up the base of her spine.
* **What they want:** To rip the tarp off the cabinet and find the physical source of that impossible signal.
* **What they fear:** That the Oracle isn't just a database, but a receiver for something much worse.
* **How they position their body:** Frozen, eyes lingering on the machine a beat too long, fingers twitching at her sides.


* **Nolan / The Vanguard**
* **What they notice first:** The acoustic shift of the crowd beyond the curtain. They are being hunted by whispers.
* **What they want:** To get off the X immediately and put the heavy steel door of the Glitch Palace behind them.
* **What they fear:** Being trapped in a dead-end room by a mob of angry, bat-wielding Nostalgists.
* **How they position their body:** Coiled tight, exhaling through his nose in relief at the coordinates, but already physically pivoting to plot an exit vector through the crowd.



### 7) THE RELIC / MACHINE / FOCUS OBJECT

The Machine Oracle has been pacified. When Alex asks the "smaller" question, the machine does not scream. Its internal fan whines and the screen jitters, but the violence is gone.

The side-mounted dot-matrix printer chatters to life. It doesn't burn the paper this time; it stamps the ink with clean, mechanical precision. The attendant tears the strip off and folds it twice, handling the data like sacred scripture. The rule of the old world holds true: if you treat the machine with reverence, it will yield its secrets without taking a pound of flesh in return.

### 8) THE UNDER-SIGNAL

The static is still here, but it has retreated to the edges of the room. Mila feels it as a faint carrier tone—a frequency that shouldn't exist in a world where the grids have been dead for thirty years. It doesn't break into the sharp, violent snaps of Velcro like before; instead, it lingers like a phantom itch.

But as the digital static recedes, the analog noise escalates. The whisper-network outside is rising to take its place. *“Who’s in there? Who did the Oracle answer?”* The pressure is no longer coming from the machine; it is coming from the people.

### 9) CHARLOTTE RADIO FRAME

"This is Charlotte, broadcasting from the Red Griffin Inn in Oakhaven. Thirty years after the Disconnect, we’re all just trying to keep what’s left from getting erased. But memory is a dangerous thing to wake up, especially in a crowded room. Keep your memories close to your chest. And when the room starts whispering your name... don't be there to answer. Keep one ear on the noise beneath the music."

### 10) PLAYER HOOK + CHOICES

The attendant hands over the folded paper. Nolan murmurs the bearing: *071 Northeast.* But outside the curtain, the storm is finally breaking. The questions are too loud.

**So tell me—what happens next?**

* **A) Lead a quiet exit.** Nolan takes the point, using subtle body language and misdirection to slip the trio quietly through the arcade crowd before the whispers curdle into a confrontation. *(Stealth/Tactical approach; low immediate risk, prioritizes escape over information).*
* **B) Step into the back room.** Mila asks the attendant for permission to step into a back room to "sketch the bearing," using the moment to secretly hunt for the source of that unnatural static. *(Tech/Investigation approach; high risk of being cornered, high reward for discovering the truth behind the anomaly).*
* **C) Perform for the crowd.** Alex holds his ground and steps out of the chamber, publicly thanking the attendant and performing a loud, vibrant "vibe" story for the nearby Nostalgists to defuse the tension. *(Social/Diplomacy approach; medium risk, relies entirely on Alex's charisma to turn a suspicious mob into an adoring audience).*
