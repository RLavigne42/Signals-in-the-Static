# Episode 83 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex takes point with the BLACKOUT YEARS binder pressed to his ribs and leads you toward DECON—not because it feels safe, but because the bunker’s own procedures are a minefield, and you’d rather step where the stenciled arrows expect feet. Behind you, the inner door holds at a 4–6 inch service gap, cam-locked. The Analog Order’s saw keeps eating the outer hatch—steady, merciless.
