# Episode 30 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex chose the slower, smarter move: *read the door like it was written by people who expected panic.* He leans in until his eyelashes almost brush the blistered paint. The bulkhead’s stencil isn’t the only message—there’s a second line half-hidden under a later patch job, the kind of thing a maintenance crew adds when the official instructions get people killed: EQUALIZE \= SELECT “B” / COUNT 20 AFTER CLICK DON’T FORCE. DON’T LIGHT. Alex traces a fingertip along a tiny serial plate riveted to the panel. The numbers are stamped deep—pre-Disconnect confidence. Next to the gauge, someone scratched three notches into the metal: |||. Not decoration. A reminder.
