# Episode 12 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex works the market the way he works a room: by making people feel like their junk-drawer history matters. You emerge from the Glitch Palace’s service alley into a side-street stitched with tarps and scavenged awnings. Neon is rare out here—just a few salvaged tubes humming weakly over stalls of vinyl sleeves, cracked Walkman shells, coiled copper, and dead circuit boards sold like charms. The air tastes like dust and fryer grease; somewhere a generator coughs and dies, leaving only the hiss of distant static. Alex keeps FRIDAY NIGHT DRIVE // 1987 in plain sight, not as bait—*as proof*. He stops at a stall where cassette spindles hang like wind chimes. The vendor is older, eyes sharp, fingers stained with oxide.
