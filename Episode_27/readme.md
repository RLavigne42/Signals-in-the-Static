# Episode 27 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan’s pebbles have barely stopped clicking when he makes his move. Up on the ridge, he palms a shard of glassy slag—black as old cassette tape, sharp as truth—and flicks it into a seam of loose rock above the patrol’s line. Then, with a second toss, he sends a heavier chunk skipping into a dry gully. The basin answers with a rattle-slide—not a full collapse, but enough to sound like something big shifting its weight. Dust blooms on the far ridge in a pale, drifting veil. The three silhouettes pause. One raises an arm. Another turns their head, scanning the wrong horizon. Nolan doesn’t watch to admire his work. He watches to *measure time*. Ten seconds, maybe twelve, before discipline reasserts itself.
