# Episode 84 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

You choose Mila. She ignores the ritual purity of spray nozzles and warning placards and goes for what still works in this dead century: an analog port and a pattern of sound. The Decon doors sit there like a mute judge—RED in the window—while, down the corridor, the Analog Order keeps worrying the outer hatch with steel and hate.
