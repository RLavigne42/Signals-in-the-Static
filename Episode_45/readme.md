# Episode 45 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

You put Nolan on the backtrail. He moves like he was born in corridors that don’t want you in them—quiet, economical, eyes always on angles. While Alex and Mila hover near the reflective tape line and the stacked crates, Nolan slips back to the P‑2 INNER door, easing it toward closed without letting the latch *clack*. He pauses with his ear to the steel. The chanting is distant—muffled by concrete—but it’s closer than it should be. And underneath it: the ugly, irregular thud-splinter of a frame being broken, not a door being opened. Nolan spits a whisper. “They’re not giving up.” He digs in his kit for anything that can *talk* without making noise: a length of crate strapping, a twist of cloth, a sliver of bent metal.
