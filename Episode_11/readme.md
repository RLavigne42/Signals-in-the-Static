# Episode 11 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex turns the decoy into a *magnet*. He lifts the newly acquired cassette over his head like it’s a trophy from a high-score duel. Neon washes over the handwritten label—FRIDAY NIGHT DRIVE // 1987—and the boombox circle answers with whoops and stomps that rattle the cabinet glass. “Listening invite,” Alex calls, loud enough for half the arcade to hear. “Back room—two minutes. If this thing plays clean, you’re all witnesses.” Witnesses. Crowd. Noise. Exactly what the Analog Order hates. Across the floor, Nolan catches a glimpse of that too-clean scout shoulder-checking the crowd, trying to find a clean line to you. He doesn’t get one. Not without shoving Nostalgists aside and starting a sanctuary brawl.
