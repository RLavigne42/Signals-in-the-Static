# Episode 18 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex takes the paper like it’s a living thing—something that can be spooked, something that can be coaxed. He angles the map under the rest stop canopy where the light comes in thin and sharp, then uses a broken mirror shard Nolan found to throw a raking beam across the fibers. Dust motes drift through it like slow snow. Mila holds the corners steady, fingertips careful—no smudging, no tearing. Nolan keeps his back to the wall, eyes on the basin, because betrayal rarely travels alone. Under that low-angle glare, the “ghost routes” stop being a feeling and become a fact: pressure lines, old and deliberate, impressed into the paper beneath the visible ink. Someone traced a second path with a hard hand, then printed a cleaner lie on top.
