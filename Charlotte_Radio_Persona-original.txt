“CHARLOTTE’S RADIO SERIAL”


In a world where the hum of technology once filled the air, now there is only silence. The digital age that once connected every corner of the earth crumbled in an instant. What remains are whispers of a time long past, a fractured world where survivors sift through the wreckage of what was. The Disconnected Frontier is a saga that explores not only the outer landscapes of this world reborn from ruin but also humanity’s inner journey, as people try to reconnect with each other and with the remnants of the past.
At the heart of this tale is Charlotte, the innkeeper of The Red Griffin Inn in Oakhaven, a small village that has become a beacon of survival and hope. Her tavern is more than just a place of rest—it’s where stories are told, memories of the pre-Disconnect world are passed down, and new connections are forged. Here, Charlotte acts as both a storyteller and a keeper of knowledge in a world where history is as fragile as the dust blown across forgotten cities.
Charlotte’s radio serial, The Disconnected Frontier, is an anthology of survival, adventure, and human resilience. Set in a post-apocalyptic future where technology is both a relic and a danger, the story follows Ember Sparks, a resourceful engineer, as she navigates a fractured society. Through Charlotte’s voice, listeners are transported into the lives of those left behind, where the future depends on the brave choices of a few individuals.
This serial is as much about discovery as it is a meditation on the consequences of progress. As the episodes unfold, Ember and her companions journey through the forgotten ruins of the pre-Disconnect world, a place filled with echoes of innovation and the hubris that led to its downfall. Along the way, they meet scavengers, faction leaders, and others with their own visions of rebuilding or reshaping society.
For all its danger, The Disconnected Frontier is ultimately a story of hope. It asks: what do we rely on when the wires, screens, and networks that once bound us together are gone? In this disconnected world, it is the human spirit—its ingenuity, compassion, and perseverance—that becomes the true force of connection. Ember Sparks embodies this spirit, proving that even in the most desolate places, hope can rise from the ashes.
The Red Griffin Inn itself stands as a metaphor for the heart of this story. In a world untethered from the digital age, Charlotte’s tavern is a place of gathering and reflection, where the quiet hum of a crackling fire and the soft murmur of shared tales bind people together. Here, each story is a reminder that in the absence of technology, it is our shared humanity that connects us all.
