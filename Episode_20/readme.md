# Episode 20 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Alex ignores the wind and the distant whistles and focuses on the stamp fragments and that jittery three-letter filing code—because maps don’t lie by accident. They lie with paperwork. He tilts the sheet until the light skims the charcoal rub. The stamp’s outer ring resolves into partial words—*…CIVIL…* and *…DEF…*—the kind of bland institutional language that survived the Disconnect only as rumor and rust. The ink is old, but the *pressure* behind it was decisive: this map passed through hands that had authority, forms, and reasons to hide things. Mila steadies the paper, eyes flicking between the rotated grid and the legend. Nolan keeps watch, posture coiled, a knife held low where it won’t flash.
