# Episode 16 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan doesn’t hesitate. He takes the maintenance culvert like it’s an old friend—dark, cramped, and honest about how badly it wants to kill you. He drops to his belly and slides into the half-buried mouth. The air inside is cooler, metallic, and wet with a sour stink—old runoff and dust that never fully settled after the world went quiet. Behind you, the Analog Order’s poles keep tapping the rubble line—*tap… tap… tap*—searching for hollows. Nolan’s choice turns that search into a guessing game. Mila follows, shoulders tight, keeping the compass and map hugged to her chest. Alex goes last, pulling the filter cloth over his satchel like a shroud, trying not to think about how paper sounds when it tears.
