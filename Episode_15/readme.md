# Episode 15 Synopsis

This episode follows the crew through a high-stakes run in *Signals in the Static*, where every choice can shift trust, danger, and momentum.

## Gameplay Synopsis

Nolan makes the call with his whole body—half a breath of “compliance” and then a sudden, violent pivot. He lets his hands rise like he’s about to open his pack… and instead he kicks a fan of glass-slag into the nearest patrolman’s shins. The crunch becomes a spray; light shatters into a thousand glittering lies. Nolan hooks Alex by the elbow and yanks him hard toward the collapsed storefront line—where broken masonry and rebar make teeth of cover. “Move,” Nolan hisses, voice flat as duct tape. Mila is already moving—she keeps the compass hidden, the map tight, her boots finding the dullest patches of ground. The Analog Order patrolman barks something like a prayer and rage combined.
